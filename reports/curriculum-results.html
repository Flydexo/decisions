06 / Reading the results

What the comparison means

Same validation questions

The latest checkpoint was evaluated locally on up to 32 validation rows per source using the run’s pinned dataset and seed settings. There are scored questions in total; Aegis and Typed Decisions ask multiple questions per row. These are validation results, not a held-out final test.

Random guessing

For each supported question, the reference gives every available option equal probability. The per-source reference is the expected accuracy, and the headline is an equal-weight mean across sources. This is a theoretical expectation, not a trained or sampled run.

Pilot baseline and previous stage

The pilot baseline used a bounded mixed training pool and its selected step-672 checkpoint. Stage 15 is the last completed curriculum checkpoint. They share the 21-source, seed-42, up-to-32-row validation protocol, but their training budgets differ.

Latest checkpoint limits

Stage 16 stopped partway through Amazon Reviews. Its downloaded recovery weights are BF16 and were evaluated with FP32 CPU operations; resuming training from this copy would start with a fresh optimizer state. A stage-14 control reproduced remote Yelp and Amazon accuracy exactly after BF16 rounding. The full optimizer checkpoint remains on a stopped Vast instance.

The 21 per-source samples are small; individual differences can be noisy. “Above chance” counts are point estimates. The -source probability note uses exploratory, unadjusted one-sided random-guess tests. Sources: .
